Welcome to Hike Los Angeles

Editor's Note:Welcome to Hike Los Angeles! Thank you for using this in-progress website. Upon noticing the lack of an equivilent site to Angeles National Forest's brother Los Padres National Forest, I created this site. Intended to create a standardized list of camps, trails, peaks, and springs, this isn't your average AllTrails clone. My hope is we can build a better understanding of the condition of trails and camps, and better them.

Hike Los Angeles is a community-built directory of trails, peaks, springs and trail camps across the mountains ringing the Los Angeles basin — the San Gabriels, the Santa Monicas, Griffith Park and beyond. Our goal is simple: catalog every trail worth walking, keep the condition reports current, and make it easy to find water, mileage and elevation before you go.

Much of the San Gabriel backcountry sees light traffic and even lighter maintenance. The more hikers who log conditions after a trip, the better we can all tell which trails are in good shape and which need a machete. If you'd like to contribute a survey after your next hike, use the Submit a Report link above.

Always carry more water than you think you'll need. Many trails listed here are on public land but are lightly maintained — check current conditions before you go.
