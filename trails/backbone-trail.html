Backbone Trail

The Backbone Trail is a 65.29-mile continuous route through the Santa Monica Mountains, from Point Mugu State Park to Will Rogers State Historic Park, stitched together from 146 named connecting trails and fire roads.

Trail Details
Length65.29 mi (146 mapped segments)
RegionSanta Monica Mountains
RoutePoint Mugu State Park to Will Rogers State Historic Park
